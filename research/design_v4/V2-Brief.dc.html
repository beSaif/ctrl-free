Why v1 looked AI-generated

Generated UIs fall back to the statistical average of every SaaS screen. Designers now call this "AI slop". The tells named most often:

  1. The same large border radius on cards, buttons, inputs and badges, so hierarchy disappears.
  2. Decorative gradients and glows, usually purple to blue.
  3. Uppercase, wide-tracked eyebrow labels above every heading.
  4. Pills and "Live"/"New" badges on things that are neither.
  5. Thick coloured left borders on rounded cards.
  6. The default font (Inter) and generic copy.

v1 had 1, 3, 4 and a soft card-on-card layout. The fix is not a better prompt but a committed system: explicit tokens, a radius scale by role, one meaningful accent, real product chrome and specific copy.

Rules all five follow

Radius0–4 px, by role. Tables and panes are square.
ColourNeutral ground plus one accent that means something (Review, or primary action). No gradients.
Type13–14 px data, tabular numerals, sentence case. No Inter, Roboto or Arial.
StatusText with a small marker, not pills.
ChromeMenus, breadcrumbs, column headers, keyboard hints, status bar.

How instructions stay grounded

Flexible Rohrep. 38 · 3.3.4 Technische Vorschriften Lüftung / Klima
«Es dürfen nur flexible Rohre bzw. Schläuche aus nichtbrennbaren Materialien verwendet werden. Der Anschluss ist aus einer mit mindestens 5mm breiten Bride aus Nitrostahl auszuführen.»
Machine translation: Only flexible pipes or hoses made of non-combustible materials may be used. The connection must use a band clamp of nitro steel at least 5 mm wide.
  1. Quote, don't summarise. The German text is copied exactly from the PDF text layer, never generated.
  2. Always cite. PDF page and section on every item, with a link that opens the page with the text highlighted.
  3. Type = the tender's own label. The topic word comes from the tender's right margin (Flexible Rohre, Filter, Brandschutzklappen), not from the model.
  4. Label everything machine-made. Translation, applies-to links and cost impact are marked as suggestions until the estimator confirms them.
  5. Check before showing. If a quote can't be found again in the PDF text, it is not shown.
