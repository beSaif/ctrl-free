Simpler screens, and the estimator owns the assumptions

Five styles from the simplest (1) to about as rich as v2 (5). Every row ends with a live assumptions screen.

What changed from v2

Too much at onceOne main task per screen. Details open on demand. No cockpits, tickers or function-key bars.
Estimator's judgmentCompetition, how much we want the job and price sensitivity are back, as the estimator's call: plain choices with the exact number shown.
Source of truthEvery changed value shows its source ("Your judgment" or "Project parameters"), who changed it and when, and an optional note such as "Heard 4 firms took the documents". Reset to default at any time.
LiveChange a value and the price, win chance and expected contribution update right away.
