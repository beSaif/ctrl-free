Ground rules
Build for any tender, not just Oberglatt
Traps in the PDF
  • No position numbers anywhere
  • One block, several sizes and quantities
  • Sports hall puts the quantity at the line start
  • Some lines have a unit but no quantity
How we stay general
  • No hardcoded pages, systems or product IDs
  • Catalogue and parameters are swappable inputs
  • Unknown item gets a status, never a forced price
  • The 22 examples are a test set, not training
Demo proof: let the jury pick any LV page, and we price it live.
